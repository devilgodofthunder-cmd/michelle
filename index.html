Michelle Calderön

Model · Miss América Internacional RM · DJ · Professional Entertainment Artist

Michelle Calderön is a Mexican figure who fuses beauty, fashion, music, and entertainment into a sophisticated and contemporary stage presence.

Her career as a professional model has led her to participate in runways, editorials, advertising campaigns, and audiovisual productions, building a versatile image that moves seamlessly between fashion, luxury, and visual communication.

Her career reached a defining milestone upon being crowned Miss América Internacional RM, a recognition that embodies her discipline, preparation, and ability to project leadership, elegance, and confidence.

As a DJ, Michelle finds another powerful form of expression through music. Her style combines energy, personality, and sensuality to create immersive experiences that connect with diverse audiences and stages.

Her experience in events, brand activations, and large-scale shows further complements her artistic profile. As a brand ambassador and host, she has participated in sports productions, motorsports, product launches, corporate events, and live entertainment, bringing interaction, presence, and an image capable of becoming an integral part of the brand experience.

Michelle Calderön represents a new generation of talent: a woman who transforms her image, her voice, and her presence into a true form of art and expression.
